FarmSaathi

FarmSaathi is a free farm assistant for farmers in Punjab, Haryana and Uttar Pradesh. Ask a question by speaking or typing in Hindi, Punjabi or English and hear the answer read aloud. Check a tomato, potato or bell pepper leaf for 15 diseases with a photo; the check runs on your phone. Type the N, P, K and pH from your Soil Health Card to see three crops that suit your field. Get the 7-day forecast for your village with advice on when to spray and when to skip watering. Read how to apply for PM-KISAN, crop insurance (PMFBY), the Kisan Credit Card, the Soil Health Card, drip irrigation subsidy (PMKSY) and e-NAM. No account is needed.
